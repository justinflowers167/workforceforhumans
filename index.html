How adaptation works

Three steps from disrupted
to your next role.

Not a course. Not a job board. A structured path from where you are to where the work is going — with a coach reading along the way.

Step 01 — Assess

Upload your resume

Drop in your resume. We read it like a coach would — naming what's strong, what's stale, and where the work is shifting underneath you. You get a clear read in about a minute.

Step 02 — Plan

Pick your pathway

A named target role — not "skills employers love." For each match we surface what to tailor on your resume, the single most-leveraged AI skill to learn next, and how to apply with the right angle for that role.

Step 03 — Move

Apply, learn, repeat

Real listings from verified employers and curated federal roles. You apply, you learn — and a Friday digest brings fresh matches scored against your pathway, each with its own coach brief.
